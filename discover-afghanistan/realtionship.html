UAE AFGHANISTAN RELATIONSHIP

BIlATERAL RELATIONS BETWEEN AFGHANISTAN
AND THE UNITED ARAB EMIRATES

1973

Afghanistan has always pursued a policy of neutrality and non-alignment in its relations with countries worldwide. The UAE and Afghanistan maintained diplomatic ties since 1973.

1973

After the invasion of the Afghan Jihad in April 1992, the Afghan Embassy was transferred to the Islamic State of Afghanistan. Mr. Noori Khan continued diplomatic duties as Chargé D’Affaires in the same year.

2002

In June 2002, the UAE renewed bilateral relations with Afghanistan. Former President Hamid Karzai visited the UAE and met UAE officials to strengthen cooperation.

2003

The Afghan Foreign Minister Dr. Abdullah visited the UAE and formed strong ties between Afghanistan and UAE for post-war rebuilding efforts.

2004

The UAE recognized the importance of relations and contributed significant humanitarian and reconstruction aid to Afghanistan.

2008

President Karzai visited UAE again in 2008 and discussed bilateral cooperation with UAE leaders, including President Sheikh Khalifa bin Zayed Al Nahyan.

2019

President Ghani expressed happiness on his visit to the UAE in March 2019, emphasizing UAE’s support for Afghan people’s progress and prosperity.
