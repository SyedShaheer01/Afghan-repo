INTRODUCTION

Discover vibrant experiences and unforgettable moments across Afghanistan.

Afghanistan, officially known as the Islamic Emirate of Afghanistan, is a landlocked country situated at the crossroads of Central and South Asia. Known as the “Heart of Asia,” it shares borders with Pakistan, Iran, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan, and China. The country covers an area of 652,864 square kilometers and is predominantly mountainous, divided by the Hindu Kush range. Kabul serves as the nation’s capital and largest city.

Human settlement in Afghanistan dates back to prehistoric times. Referred to as the “graveyard of empires,” Afghanistan has witnessed numerous invasions and empires including the Persians, Greeks under Alexander the Great, Maurya Empire, Mongols, and the British. In more recent history, it experienced foreign interventions from the Soviet Union and the United States.

The modern state of Afghanistan began with the Durrani Empire in the 18th century. Over the years, the region has endured countless conflicts but has remained a central cultural and historical hub in Asia. Today, Afghanistan continues to rebuild while preserving its deep-rooted traditions and heritage.

Capital

The capital city of Afghanistan is Kabul.

Population

Afghanistan has a diverse population with major ethnic groups such as Pashtuns, Tajiks, Hazaras, and Uzbeks.

Languages

Pashto and dari are the official language..

Religion

Islam is the predominant religion, with most Afghans being Sunni Muslims.

The ancient centers of culture and civilization were influenced by diverse outsiders such as Greece, Arabia, Iran, Central Asia, India, and China. The borders of Afghanistan were established in 1893 through negotiations with the British, and provincial governments emerged, taking the place of clan rule.
