The Afghan Consulate - Dubai

Representing Afghanistan, serving the Afghan community

We are committed to protecting the rights and interests of Afghan nationals, strengthening ties between Afghanistan and the UAE, and facilitating cultural, social, and economic cooperation between the two countries.

Our team works to ensure that every Afghan citizen abroad has access to timely, and transparent consular services.

Our Mission

To serve Afghans abroad with
dignity, professionalism, and
cultural pride, while strengthening Afghanistan’s diplomatic and community presence in the UAE.

Our Vision

To build a trusted and modern consular system that connects Afghans abroad with their homeland and ensures they are never without support.

Our Responsibilities

The Consulate is responsible for:

Issuing and renewing passports
Processing Afghan visas for foreigners
Providing document attestation & legalization
Handling birth, marriage, and death registrations
Offering emergency legal and travel assistance
Supporting refugees and Afghan nationals in need
Issuing and renewing passports
Promoting Afghan cultural and educational exchange

MESSAGE FROM THE CONSUL GENERAL

The Consulate is responsible for:

Our duty is not just administrative — it is personal. Every service we provide is a connection between Afghans in the UAE and their homeland. We are here to protect your rights, assist your needs, and represent Afghanistan with dignity.

Abdul Rahman Fida

Consul General of Afghanistan – Dubai

Jurisdiction & Community

The Consulate General of Afghanistan in Dubai serves Afghan nationals living across Dubai and the Northern Emirates.

Today, more than 150,000 Afghans call the UAE their home — making this consulate a vital link between the Afghan diaspora and Afghanistan.
