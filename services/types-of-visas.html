Types of Visas

There are different types of visa to apply. Please read the information on each type carefully and select the right one. Applicants for an Afghan visa, regardless of the type of visa, are required to obey the laws of the Islamic Republic of Afghanistan.

Single Entry Visa

This type of visa is issued by the diplomatic and consular missions of Afghanistan abroad. This visa is issued with the validity of three months and a staying time of one month after an approval letter from the Ministry of Foreign Ministry.

This type of visa can be changed to other types of visas including a Work Visa or can be extended once the holder enters Afghanistan with the approval of the Ministry of Interior.

Multiple–Entry Visa

This type of visa is issued by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Ministry of Interior Affairs in Afghanistan and the Afghan diplomatic and consular missions abroad to the heads and employees of foreign organizations and companies, flight crews, foreign service workers and drivers, with the validity and staying time of three months to one year for multiple entries based.

Non-Work-Entry Visa

This type of visa is issued to foreign nationals who travel to Afghanistan to study, settle legal and business transactions, visiting family, doing academic research, and media and cultural affairs and sports work, or to attend academic conferences and seminars, and to attend formal invitations by the Islamic Republic of Afghanistan. This visa is issued with the validity of three months and a staying time of one month.

In exceptional cases, if necessary, this visa is extended by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Afghanistan.

Stay Visa

This type of visa can be issued by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the Ministry of Interior Affairs in Afghanistan to those foreign nationals who hold a Single–Entry Visa, Work Visa, Non–Work Entry Visa, Tourist Visa or Transit Visa. This visa is issued with the validity and staying time of one to two years and can be extended upon request.

Applicants can apply for this type of visa through the diplomatic or consular missions of Afghanistan abroad provided that they are not already inside the country via a valid visa.

Transit Visa

This type of visa is issued by the diplomatic and consular missions of Afghanistan abroad or in exceptional situations by the airport authorities. This visa is issued to foreign nationals who travel through Afghanistan.

The validity of this visa is one month, and the staying time in case of air travel is 72 hours; the staying time in case of land travel is 6 days starting from the date of entry. This visa cannot be extended except in emergency cases (death of a travel companion, unexpected incidents, road closures, or closure of the origin or destination airport).
