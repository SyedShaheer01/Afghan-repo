Required Documents

Application form completed in Dari/Pashto and English

Passport copy

National ID copy

Residence permit copy

Children

If the applicant is under the legal age of 18 years, additionally the following documents are required:

a. Parents’ passports copy

b. Parent’s marriage certificate

Time

A birth certificate will be issued on same day if requirements are met.

Fees

The fee for a birth certificate amounts to 10 USD.
